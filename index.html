    How a battle works

    Battles take about a minute. You stand behind your hero, facing the enemy, and your answers do the fighting.

    1. A question drops

      Four answers and eight seconds, with a ring of light running down around the question.

    2. Answer right and you strike

      Answer in the first few seconds and the hit lands half as hard again. Get one wrong or run out of time and the enemy hits back.

    3. Three in a row unleash your special

      Kael's Eclipse Verse, Rin's Overload Arc or Akane's Crimson Requiem, each two and a half times as strong as a normal strike.

    Come back every day

    Each day one of the world bosses returns as the daily boss, with fresh questions. Finish a battle each day to keep your streak going, and save freezes to cover a day you miss.

    Along the way you earn titles like Quick Draw, Unbroken and Week of Fire, and dress your hero in auras and card frames. Everything you collect is earned by playing.

    Made for curious players

    • Six topics

      Science and Space, Geography, Animals and Nature, History, Sport, and Games and Tech.

    • No chat, no accounts

      Nobody can message you, and the game never asks for your name, email or photo.

    • Break reminders

      After 45 minutes of play, the game suggests a break. You can turn this off in settings.
