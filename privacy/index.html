Privacy policy

For the Quizenshi game and this website. Last updated 15 November 2026.

The short version

  • You don't need an account. The game never asks for your name, email, age or photo.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone, not with us.
  • The game sends us play stats, like "a battle was won in Shadow Academy". They carry no name or ID that identifies you, and you can switch them off in Settings.
  • This version of the game shows no ads and has nothing to buy.
  • There's no chat, so nobody can message you.
  • The game never asks for your phone's location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.
  • We never sell your information.
  • This website has no cookies, no tracking and no ads.

Who we are

Quizenshi is made by Loaders Studios Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17225067), with its registered office as shown on the Companies House register. We are responsible for the information described here. If you have a question, email support@quizenshi.com.

Who the game is for

Quizenshi is for players aged 13 and over. Because many players are teenagers, we've kept it simple and private: there is no chat, no way to share anything you write, no account, and nothing that follows you around the internet. If you're under 18, it's a good idea to read this with a parent or carer.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress on your phone so you can carry on where you left off. That includes:

  • the worlds and stages you've cleared, your stars, team level and experience, how many times you've lost each stage since you last won it (so the game can make its questions a little easier), and your best floor in the endless tower;
  • your heroes, coins, titles, auras and frames, and which of them you've already seen in the collection;
  • your daily streak, freezes, reward track and whether you've beaten today's boss;
  • how many questions you've got right and wrong, how many you've got right in each topic, your current run of right answers, and which questions you've seen recently so they don't come round again too soon;
  • how many battles and sessions you've played, and the day you first played;
  • when, and how many times, the game has asked the App Store or Google Play to show their rating box, and the day you last sent your idea votes;
  • your settings: sound, music, vibration, reduce flashing, break reminders and Share play stats.

This save is not sent to us. If part of it is ever damaged, the game keeps a copy of the damaged part on your phone too. It stays until you use Reset progress in the game's settings or delete the game. It may also be included in your phone's own backup, such as iCloud or Google backup, which we can't see.

On the screens where you pick a world or look at a hero card, the game reads your phone's motion sensor so the pictures tilt as you move it, unless your phone's reduce motion setting is on. The readings stay on your phone and are never saved or sent.

Play stats: what the game sends us

To find out what's fun, what's too hard and what's broken, the game sends short messages about how it's being played. We call these play stats. A privacy-focused analytics service, Aptabase, collects them for us and stores them in the European Union.

WhenWhat is sent
The game opens or comes back on screenJust that it happened
The tutorial endsHow many seconds it took
A battle starts or endsThe world and stage, whether it was a stage, the daily boss or a floor of the endless tower, and at the end the result, stars, seconds taken, and how many answers were right and wrong
You leave the gameHow long you played and which screen you were on

Every message also carries the time it was sent, how many days since you first played, the kind of phone system and its version (for example iOS 26), the game's version and build number, the version of Aptabase's code, a fixed language code that is the same for everyone, and a session number made from the time the game started plus random digits. The session number changes whenever the game restarts, or after an hour without play.

We never send your name, anything you write, any device ID or any advertising ID. Aptabase uses your phone's internet address to work out the country and region a message came from, and to count how many different players there were each day, using a code made from the address, the phone's system and version, and a value that is thrown away every 24 hours. The internet address itself is not stored. Aptabase keeps the messages for up to five years.

The Share play stats switch

Share play stats is on when you first play, and you can switch it off at any time in Settings. While it's off, the game sends no play stats at all.

We leave it on to start with because play stats say nothing about who you are, and without them we couldn't spot a stage that's too hard, battles that drag on, or a part of the game that's broken. UK law allows this kind of counting without asking first, as long as it is only used to improve the game and you can switch it off for free at any time.

Because the messages contain nothing that identifies you, we can't pick out the messages that came from one particular player, so we can't show them to you or delete them on request. Switching off Share play stats stops any more being sent.

We use play stats because we have a legitimate interest in understanding how the game is played so we can make it better and fairer.

Messages you choose to send

  • Vote for what comes next. In Settings you can pick up to three ideas for new things in the game. Each vote holds only the idea you picked and how many days since you first played. There's no text box.

These go through Aptabase like play stats, with the same extra details. They're sent even when Share play stats is off, because you chose to send them. They leave your phone within about a minute, as long as it's online. We use them because we have a legitimate interest in making the things players want.

Ads and purchases

This version of Quizenshi shows no ads and has nothing to buy. The code for ads and an optional membership is already in the game, ready for a later update, but this version never asks it to show an ad or sell anything. Before ads or a membership arrive, we'll update this policy and the app store privacy labels first.

The rating box

Now and then, after a good moment in the game, the game asks the App Store or Google Play to show their own rating box. The store decides whether it appears. Anything you choose to give there goes to Apple or Google, not to us, though a review you post on the store is public and we can read and reply to it.

Crash reports from Apple and Google

If you've allowed it in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may share crash reports with us. They don't tell us who you are, and we use them only to fix problems.

What the game never does

  • It never asks for your name, email, age or photo, and it has no account.
  • It never asks for your phone's location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.
  • It never uses the advertising ID and never asks to track you across apps.
  • It has no chat and no text boxes, so nothing you write is ever sent.
  • It never sells your information or builds a profile of you.
  • The versions in the App Store and Google Play never download updates on their own. Every update comes through the store, so the game doesn't contact any other update service.

If you email us

If you email support@quizenshi.com, we'll see your email address and whatever you write. Our email is run by Google Workspace. We use your message only to reply to you, and we delete it, with our reply, 12 months after we last wrote to each other. Please don't send more personal information than you need to.

This website

quizenshi.com has no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no forms, and it loads nothing from other companies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. When you visit, GitHub logs your internet address and stores it for security purposes, whether or not you have a GitHub account. We don't see these records. GitHub's privacy statement explains how it handles them.

Where information is kept

Play stats and idea votes are stored by Aptabase in the European Union, which UK law accepts as protecting personal information to UK standards. Google and GitHub are based in the United States and may handle information there and in other countries. They decide how to protect what they collect, and explain this in their own privacy policies. Our support email is kept by Google Workspace, which works for us under its data processing terms, including when it keeps email outside the UK.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. We'll answer within one month. You can object to play stats at any time by switching off Share play stats. Play stats and idea votes carry nothing we could use to find yours, and your progress is on your own phone where you control it. If you've emailed us, we can find and delete your emails. Apple and Google also have their own privacy settings and tools for what they hold.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, please tell us first at support@quizenshi.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change how the game uses information, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the game.
